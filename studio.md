## Intro

TeloNexus Studio is the creative arm of Karabo Nexus Group. The division crafts logos, brand identity, and marketing collateral for clients across food, services, events, and more — and converts physical artwork into clean digital files.

Every piece is built to communicate clearly.

## Capabilities

- Logo design and brand identity packages
- Flyers, posters, and menus
- Business cards, letterhead, receipts and payslips
- Social media graphics
- Digitization: clean copy, retouching, restoration, and vector rebuilding

## Digitization

Bringing drawings, sketches, artwork, and old photos into the digital world — as a clean hi-res copy, a restored original, or a scalable vector.

| Finish | Price (BWP) | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| Clean copy | 80 | Tidy, hi-res digital image |
| Retouch / enhance | 150 | Remove stains, scratches, fix light |
| Restoration | 250 | Repair damaged / aged originals |
| Vector rebuild | 300 | Trace into scalable vector, logo-ready |

**How it works:** Send it over → We digitize it → You get the files (print-ready, screen-ready, web-ready).

Batches of digitized pieces qualify for volume discounts: 5+ pieces 10% off; 10+ pieces 15% off.
