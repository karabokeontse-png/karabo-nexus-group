The name

The word is answer.

Karabo — the name we carry — means "answer" in Setswana. It is the idea the group is built on: that good work is judged by what it resolves for the people who asked for it.

Karabo Nexus Group brings together the practices needed to deliver that answer in full — from the code that runs a system, to the design that explains it, to the audit that keeps it reliable, to the page that publishes it.
