Labs and Advisory fail at 16px. On a 128-unit grid their strokes render at 0.50px and 0.40px; a 1px line needs 8 units. Both collapse into broken hairlines and their node dots fall under 1.2px. A dedicated small-size variant is required — scaling down is not enough. Studio and Print pass, being filled shapes rather than line art.
