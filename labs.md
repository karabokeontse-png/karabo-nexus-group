## Intro

TeloNexus Labs is the software engineering arm of Karabo Nexus Group. The division designs and builds websites, web applications, and e-commerce platforms, from single-page launches to multi-module systems with role-based access and payment integration.

All code — presentation, logic, and data — is built and maintained here.
